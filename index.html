THE REASON BEHIND THE RANKING

Four zones. A clearer starting point.

Editorial planning categories based on industrial concentration, cargo mix and distribution connectivity. These are not official ratings, measured freight volumes or guaranteed advertising results.

01 / HIGHER

Start testing here

Dense manufacturing or distribution clusters with a broad potential shipper base. Strong starting points for focused campaigns; competition may also be high.

02 / HIGH

Target the industry

Established industrial locations. Match your message to the local cargo, shipment size and lanes you can serve.

03 / MEDIUM

Validate a specific lane

Regional manufacturing and distribution opportunities. Confirm dispatch frequency and return-load economics before scaling.

04 / LOW

Use selective outreach

Selected urban retail cores with less obvious fit for broad factory-freight ads. Nearby industrial belts may rank differently.

Run a focused test.
Measure real shipments.

  1. Choose one cluster. Match pickup coverage and delivery lanes.
  2. Qualify enquiries. Ask company, cargo, weight, pincodes and dispatch date.
  3. Measure outcomes. Compare quotes, bookings and margin, alongside lead cost.
Sources, methodology & limitations

Curated October 2026. This is a representative shortlist, not a census. Coordinates indicate approximate cluster centres, not geographic boundaries. Zone ratings and PTL/FTL suitability are editorial estimates, not sourced demand scores. Verify pickup coverage, cargo restrictions, vehicle availability and economics with operations. Low applies only to the selected urban core, not the whole state. No live freight or ad performance data is connected. PTL: consolidated part load. FTL: dedicated truck.
